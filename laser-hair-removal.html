About the Treatment

Laser Hair Removal

Unwanted hair on the face and body can be a common concern for many people. Regular shaving, waxing, and threading may require frequent maintenance and can sometimes lead to skin irritation, ingrown hairs, or discomfort. Laser hair removal is a popular cosmetic procedure that helps reduce unwanted hair over time, offering a longer-lasting alternative to traditional hair removal methods.

The treatment uses concentrated light energy that is absorbed by the pigment in the hair and converted into heat, which helps damage the hair follicles and reduce future hair growth. If you are considering laser hair removal in South Delhi, a professional skin assessment can help determine the appropriate treatment settings based on your skin tone, hair colour, and the area being treated. Laser hair reduction can be performed on areas such as the face, upper lip, chin, underarms, arms, legs, and other suitable body areas. Multiple sessions are usually needed because hair grows in different cycles, and results vary from person to person.

At Derma Plus Clinic (Dr. Manisha Chopra), patients can seek professional guidance on laser hair removal and suitable hair reduction options. The treatment plan is tailored to individual needs, with attention to skin type, hair growth patterns, and treatment goals. Temporary redness, swelling, or sensitivity may occur after a session. Following the recommended aftercare instructions and protecting the skin from excessive sun exposure are important for recovery.

Results and suitability vary from person to person. Please book a consultation for a personal assessment.
