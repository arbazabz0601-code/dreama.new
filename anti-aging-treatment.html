About the Treatment

Anti-Aging Treatment

As the skin ages, it gradually loses collagen, elasticity, and natural moisture, which can lead to fine lines, wrinkles, sagging, and changes in skin texture. Sun exposure, pollution, lifestyle habits, and genetics can also influence how the skin changes over time. Anti-aging treatments are designed to address these concerns, improve the appearance of the skin, and help maintain a smoother, firmer, and more refreshed look.

Modern dermatology offers several anti-aging treatment options depending on an individual's skin condition and concerns. These may include chemical peels, Botox, dermal fillers, HIFU, laser treatments, and other skin rejuvenation procedures. If you are looking for anti-aging treatment in South Delhi, a personalized skin assessment can help identify suitable options for reducing the appearance of fine lines, improving skin texture, and addressing age-related changes. The most appropriate treatment depends on factors such as skin type, age, skin laxity, and desired results.

At Derma Plus Clinic (Dr. Manisha Chopra), patients can seek professional guidance on anti-aging treatments tailored to their skin concerns. The dermatologist evaluates the skin and discusses suitable procedures, expected outcomes, possible side effects, and aftercare requirements. Depending on the treatment selected, some improvements may be noticeable quickly, while others develop gradually over time. Maintaining a consistent skincare routine, using sunscreen daily, and following professional advice can also help protect the skin from premature ageing.

Results and suitability vary from person to person. Please book a consultation for a personal assessment.
