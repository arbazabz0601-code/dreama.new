About the Doctor

Dr. Manisha Chopra

"Every skin has its own story — my approach is to listen first, then treat with precision and care."

Dr. Manisha Chopra is the founder of Derma Plus, a trusted skin, hair and laser clinic in Greater Kailash-II, New Delhi. With 18+ years of clinical experience in dermatology and cosmetology, she follows a personalised and evidence-based approach to skin and hair care. At Derma Plus, patients receive individualised guidance and treatment plans based on their specific skin, hair and aesthetic concerns. The clinic provides a modern, well-equipped clinical environment where patients can make informed decisions about their dermatological and cosmetic care with professional guidance.
